Campaign map // levels 1–50

Fifty nodes.

The campaign starts in the lobby at 22:17 and ends at the city core. Levels 1–10 are a continuous escape through the tower. From Level 11, each chapter opens into missions built around investigation, evidence, a defensive decision and a boss. It is all one campaign.

Quick answers

How many levels are in Cyber Access Protocol?

Fifty levels across five chapters. Levels 1–10 are a continuous run through the tower; Levels 11–50 are forty standalone missions.

How long does the campaign take?

Most levels take 8–15 minutes and each chapter about 90–150 minutes. A typical session is 30–120 minutes.

Does every level have a boss?

Every mission from Level 11 ends with a boss, and each of the first ten levels builds to its own chapter boss.

Can I replay a mission?

Yes. The Level Archive lets you revisit any level you have reached, with practice and replay options for every mission.
