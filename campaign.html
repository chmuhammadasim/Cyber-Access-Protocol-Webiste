Campaign map // levels 1–70

Seventy nodes.
One living record.

The campaign starts in the lobby at 22:17 and ends in the AEGIS Operations Campus. Levels 1–10 are a continuous escape through the tower. From Level 11, each chapter opens into missions built around investigation, evidence, a configuration decision and a boss. It is all one campaign.

Quick answers

How many levels are in Cyber Access Protocol?

Seventy levels across seven chapters. Levels 1–10 are a continuous run through the tower; Levels 11–70 are sixty standalone missions, ending in the AEGIS Operations Campus.

How long does the campaign take?

Most levels take 8–15 minutes and each chapter about 90–150 minutes. The full seventy-level campaign is a multi-evening operation.

Does every level have a boss?

Every mission from Level 11 ends with a boss, and each of the first ten levels builds to its own chapter boss. Chapters VI and VII add twenty new boss archetypes.

Can I replay a mission?

Yes. The Level Archive lets you revisit any level you have reached, with practice and replay options for every mission. V7 certifications and endings are stored separately.
