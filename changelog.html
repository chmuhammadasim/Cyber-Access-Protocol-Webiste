Chapter by chapter

Five releases. One continuous campaign.

CHAPTER V 16 November 2026 Finale

The Last Signal — Levels 41–50

The tower opens onto the city. Chapter V turns the campaign outward: intelligence, hunting, forensics, purple teaming, zero-day response, resilience, AI oversight, insider risk and incident command all converge on one adversary.

  • Ten final missions, from the intelligence exchange to the city core.
  • Nine security disciplines, each with its own decisions and field doctrine.
  • The Evidence Workbench: the evidence you gather becomes searchable, and the Knowledge Library unlocks as you play.
  • Your record carries forward — a strong operation can change the next mission's opening and reveal cross-reference evidence.
  • Ten new bosses, from the Signal Collector to the Null Architect.
  • Three endings: Coordinated Defense, Controlled Automation or Full Shutdown.
  • Seventy original painterly environments, giving every mission its own palette and architecture.

CHAPTER IV 13 November 2026

Data Apocalypse — Levels 31–40

Asterion's data estate leaves the building. Chapter IV moves through cloud accounts, exposed storage, key vaults, containers, build pipelines, offline backups, vulnerability markets, supply chains and the compliance archive.

  • Ten cloud and data missions, each with its own incident and boss.
  • A colder visual language — ice blues, electric highlights and data motifs.
  • Recovery is a theme, not a button: backups, restore order and data governance decide the chapter's ending.
  • Ten data-era bosses, from the Control Plane to Data Sovereign.
10 missions 10 bosses Levels 31–40
CHAPTER III 13 November 2026

Identity Wars — Levels 21–30

The fight moves from cables to credentials. Chapter III is about who is allowed in, what they may touch and how quickly that permission can be taken away.

  • Ghost accounts, directory inheritance, second factors, federation trusts and just-in-time privilege.
  • Machine identities and secrets living past their owners.
  • Sessions revoked, roles recertified and zero trust rebuilt one request at a time.
  • Ten identity bosses in violet and magenta, from the Forgotten User to Proxy Heir.
10 missions 10 bosses Levels 21–30
CHAPTER II 12 November 2026

Dark Network — Levels 11–20

The campaign leaves the tower's lower floors and follows the signal into a carrier hub, a resolver vault, a router plant and the trust boundaries between networks.

  • Ten network missions, each with a full investigation and debrief.
  • DNS mirages, routing labyrinths, remote-access tunnels, wireless shadows and certificate cities.
  • A Level Archive that remembers every mission, and a practice mode for the console work.
  • Three boss families: the Path Warden, the Packet Hound and the Null Sentinel.
10 missions 3 boss families Levels 11–20
CHAPTER I 11 November 2026

The Breach — Levels 1–10

At 22:17, Asterion Tower locks down. Alex Vale starts with a Pulse Tool, a scanner and limited authority — and has to reach the building's systems before the incident reaches the AI core.

  • Ten continuous levels and fifty rooms, from the night-shift lobby to the final firewall.
  • Passwords, malware, servers, network segmentation, phishing, identity, data, surveillance and incident response.
  • Ten chapter bosses, including the Login Phantom, Virus King, Phisher, Shadow Root and CyberCore.
  • English and Urdu, available from the first chapter.

Where it stands

The campaign is complete.

All fifty levels and forty missions are playable from the lobby to the city core. The next work is polish, a recorded soundtrack and bringing the game to more players. No store release date or price has been announced.
